🌟 Let's learn superlatives!

An adjective describes something. A superlative adjective compares three or more things and tells us which has the most of a quality.

Add -estfast → fastest
tall → tallest
Change spellingheavy → heaviest
large → largest
Use mostdangerous → most dangerous
Say “the most dangerous”.

10 animal friends

🐘 Elephant

heavy→heaviest

The elephant is the heaviest land animal.

Heavy means weighing a lot. Heaviest means weighing more than the others.

🐻 Bear

strong→strongest

The bear is the strongest animal in the group.

Strong means powerful. Strongest means more powerful than the others in this group.

🐇 Rabbit

fast→fastest

The rabbit is the fastest animal in the race.

Fast means moving quickly. Fastest means quicker than everyone else in this race.

🐸 Frog

small→smallest

The frog is the smallest animal in the group.

Small means little in size. Smallest means less big than the others in this group.

🐼 Panda

cute→cutest

The panda is the cutest animal.

Cute means adorable. Cutest is an opinion: different people may choose different animals.

🦒 Giraffe

tall→tallest

The giraffe is the tallest land animal.

Tall describes height. Tallest means greater in height than all other living land animals.

🐊 Crocodile

dangerous→most dangerous

The crocodile is the most dangerous animal in the group.

Dangerous means able to cause harm. Most dangerous means posing the greatest risk in this group.

🦤 Ostrich

large→largest

The ostrich is the largest bird in the group.

Large means big. Largest means bigger than the other birds. Ostriches are the largest living birds.

🦇 Bat

light→lightest

The bat is the lightest animal in the group.

Light means not heavy. Lightest means weighing the least in this group.

🐍 Snake

long→longest

The snake is the longest animal in the group.

Long means stretching far from end to end. Longest means longer than the others in this group.
