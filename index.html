🌟 First, let's learn!

An adjective describes an animal. A superlative adjective helps us compare three or more animals or things and say which has the most or least of a quality.

1 · Add -est

tall → tallest
fast → fastest
long → longest

2 · Watch the spelling

heavy → heaviest
cute → cutest
large → largest

3 · Use most

dangerous → most dangerous
Remember: the most dangerous

Grammar tip: Superlatives usually come after the: “the tallest land animal”.

10 animal friends
EXPLORE 01

🐘 Elephant

heavy→heaviest

The elephant is the heaviest land animal.

💡 Heavy means weighing a lot. Heaviest means weighing the most.

EXPLORE 02

🐻 Bear

strong→strongest

The bear is the strongest animal in the group.

💡 Strong describes power. Strongest means having the most strength in the group.

EXPLORE 03

🐇 Rabbit

fast→fastest

The rabbit is the fastest animal in the race.

💡 Fast means moving quickly. Fastest means moving more quickly than the others in this race.

EXPLORE 04

🐸 Frog

small→smallest

The frog is the smallest animal in the group.

💡 Small describes size. Smallest means less big than all the other animals in the group.

EXPLORE 05

🐼 Panda

cute→cutest

The panda is the cutest animal.

💡 Cute means adorable. Cutest is an opinion and different people may choose differently.

EXPLORE 06

🦒 Giraffe

tall→tallest

The giraffe is the tallest land animal.

💡 Tall describes height. Tallest means reaching the greatest height.

EXPLORE 07

🐊 Crocodile

dangerous→most dangerous

The crocodile is the most dangerous animal in the group.

💡 Dangerous means it could cause harm. Most dangerous compares the greatest risk in a group.

EXPLORE 08

🦤 Ostrich

large→largest

The ostrich is the largest bird in the group.

💡 Large means big. Largest means the biggest in the group.

EXPLORE 09

🦇 Bat

light→lightest

The bat is the lightest animal in the group.

💡 Light means not weighing much. Lightest means weighing the least.

EXPLORE 10

🐍 Snake

long→longest

The snake is the longest animal in the group.

💡 Long describes distance from end to end. Longest means having the greatest length.
